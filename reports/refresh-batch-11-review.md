## Flags that need your decision

- **Regrow in 3 weeks (compliance):** the old post's framing implied quick natural regrowth. No source supports a 3-week timeline, so the rewrite says so and uses hair-cycle, seasonal-shedding and nutritional-timing research.
- **Biotin timing:** the keyword asks "how long", but no study establishes a timeline in healthy people. The article says that and covers the two reviews plus the lab-test interference guidance.
- **Ashwagandha (safety):** includes a 2026 scoping review of liver injury (25 patients, one transplant, three deaths among people with cirrhosis). Stated plainly. The only hair trial is a small topical serum study.
- **Selenium:** studies point opposite ways (lower in alopecia areata, higher in chronic telogen effluvium). Both are shown. One source (Sutter 2008, selenosis case report) has no abstract in PubMed, so the article reports only its title.
- **Title-only sources (no abstract in PubMed):** Sutter 2008 (selenium), Buontempo 2023 (density) and Mysore 2007 (trichologist). Each article says so.
- **Trichologist:** all three sources are opinion pieces (a dermatologist viewpoint, a trichologist's defence, an editorial). The article labels them and only advises asking about training.
- **Blends, not single ingredients:** `amino-acids-for-hair-growth` rests on three branded blend trials (no brand named), and the article says the amino acids cannot be credited.
- **Medical mentions (neutral):** alopecia areata, androgenetic alopecia, telogen effluvium, B12 deficiency, liver disease.
- **General advice not from the studies:** a few lines (compare photos over time, tell your doctor about every supplement) are general; see each `review.md`.
