## Hair Revival Serum (/products/hair-growth-serum)
- **Does hair serum really grow hair?** A scalp serum with tested ingredients can support fuller-looking hair over time; shine serums for the lengths of your hair don't. Hair Revival Serum is a leave-on scalp serum with rosemary oil, caffeine and adenosine, ingredients studied in people with thinning hair.
- **How long until I see results?** Give it at least 90 days of nightly use. Hair grows about half an inch a month, so changes show slowly.
- **How do I use it?** Apply to a clean, dry scalp at night, parting your hair to reach thinning areas. Massage it in and leave it on.
- **Can I use it with minoxidil?** Many people do. Ask your doctor or pharmacist first, and introduce one product at a time.
- **Is it suitable for all hair types?** Yes. It works on the scalp, so it suits straight, curly and coily hair.

## Derma Roller (/products/derma-roller)
- **What needle length is it?** 1 mm, with stainless steel needles.
- **How often should I use it?** Once a week, on a clean scalp. Don't use it on irritated, broken or infected skin.
- **Can I use it with a serum?** Yes. Roll first, then apply your scalp serum (unless the serum's instructions say otherwise).
- **How do I keep it clean?** Clean it after every use, store it in its case, and replace it regularly. Never share it.
- **Is it right for a sensitive scalp?** 1 mm needles are on the longer side for home use. If your scalp is sensitive, roll gently with light pressure, and stop if you get redness that lasts or any bleeding.
