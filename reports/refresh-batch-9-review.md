## Flags that need your decision

- **Strongest hair evidence:** `palmetto` (systematic review, a 2-year comparison with finasteride where saw palmetto did less well, and a placebo-controlled trial), `pine-bark` (one randomized trial in menopausal women), `hair-growth-supplements-for-men` (a 2025 network meta-analysis).
- **Old claims removed:** 'DHT blocker for hair loss prevention' (palmetto), 'Hair Regrowth' and 'prevent hair loss' (pine bark), 'Clinically Proven Results' (men's supplements), dandruff and antimicrobial claims (turmeric), DIY masks and dosing (ginkgo).
- **Medical mentions (neutral):** androgenetic alopecia, telogen effluvium, alopecia areata, menopause, and finasteride named only as a study comparator with a doctor referral.
- **Fine hair:** the 'handle hair gently' tip is labelled as general advice, not from the studies.
