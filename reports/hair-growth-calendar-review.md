# Hair-growth content calendar review

Reviewed the 26 posts in `data/reference/hair_growth_content_calendar.csv` (published 20 September–14 October 2026) against index status, Search Console, keyword demand, compliance wording, citations and overlap with older hair-growth posts. Read-only: nothing was changed.

## Summary

1. **The structure is good.** All 26 are live; 24 are indexed (the two newest are discovered, not yet indexed). Every supporting article links to its pillar, and none has AI-chat leftovers.
2. **They're too new to judge on traffic:** 1 click and 895 impressions so far (Search Console data runs to 9 October).
3. **Product naming is inconsistent.** 18 of the 26 posts (and no other published article) promote "FullyVital's Density Scalp Treatment", but link to `/products/hair-growth-serum`. That page and your product sheet call it the Hair Growth Serum or Hair Revival Serum. Inconsistent product names confuse readers, and they confuse search and AI systems trying to match a product to its page. The Scandinavian Biolabs comparison also prices it at "$55 (Density Scalp Treatment)"; check that price is current.
4. **The claims are mostly structure/function** ("designed to support the scalp"). Two posts say the serum is "designed to target the DHT pathway". DHT-blocking is how prescription hair-loss drugs work, so that wording edges toward a drug claim. Consider "supports a healthy scalp environment".
5. **6 posts cite no studies** (several telogen-effluvium and postpartum spokes), and none follows your 3-study citation style yet. These are candidates for a light citation pass, not a rewrite.
6. **Some posts overlap with older posts on the same topic:**
   - the new telogen effluvium pillar vs the older `/telogen-effluvium` (14 clicks);
   - `/is-this-normal-shedding` vs the older `/how-much-hair-loss-is-normal`;
   - `/diffuse-thinning-vs-pattern-baldness` vs `/diffuse-alopecia`;
   - `/prenatal-vitamins-hair-growth` vs `/biotin-vs-prenatal-vitamins-for-hair-growth` (217 clicks, already refreshed).
7. **The demand is solid for the big pillars:** postpartum hair loss (about 14,800 a month in DataForSEO), how much hair loss is normal (about 5,400) and prenatal vitamins and hair (about 5,400). Many spoke queries have no measurable volume, as you noted in the calendar.
