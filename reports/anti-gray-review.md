# Anti-gray content review and gray-hair hub plan

Reviewed the 66 articles in `data/reference/anti_gray_content_calendar.csv` plus 6 other 2026 gray-hair posts, against Search Console, index status, Phase 2 keyword demand, the compliance rules and the October AI Overview baseline. Read-only: nothing was changed.

## Summary

1. **The structure is good.** 66 of 66 are live; 56 are indexed. 49 of 51 supporting articles link to their pillar, every article cites studies, and none has AI-chat leftovers.
2. **Traffic is still very small:** 97 clicks and 10,212 impressions in total since June. Some of that is age; most is demand (point 3).
3. **Most clusters target searches almost nobody makes.** Comparisons, reviews, system and routine, product deep-dives, dating, texture, and the 8 age-and-gender listicles target queries with little or no measurable volume. The big gray searches sit mostly in clusters where FullyVital has no clear hub: what causes gray hair (about 47,340/month), gray hair in men (12,100 for "greying men" alone), and B12 and gray hair (4,400).
4. **The biggest risk is a repeated survey claim.** 66 of the 66 calendar articles (and 0 of the other 6) carry a variant of "In an April 2025 FullyVital customer survey, 85% of users reported noticing fewer grays after 90 days" or "88% saw new pigment at the roots within 60 days". Repeated on almost every page, a self-reported customer survey is unlikely to substantiate an efficacy claim, and "new pigment at the roots" reads as a repigmentation claim. This is your call; I haven't edited it.
5. **10 titles or meta descriptions use reverse, prevent or treatment wording.** Several are honest question formats ("Can Gray Hair Be Reversed?"), but "Premature Gray Hair Reversal: Why Early Action Works Best" and the reversal-study title read as promises next to product widgets.
6. **Some pages duplicate each other:**
   - three pages answer "what causes gray hair";
   - two glutathione-for-gray-hair pages (one is the refreshed `/hair-vitamins/glutathione`);
   - two "which gray-hair ingredients work" pages;
   - three review pages that split "does FullyVital work" searches between them.
7. **AI Overviews already dominate these searches:** 40 of 43 priority searches show one, and FullyVital is cited in none. Arey is the only brand regularly cited.

## AEO/GEO standard for every hub

- **Answer first:** a 40–60 word direct answer under each question heading, then the evidence.
- **A short evidence table** where useful: cause or ingredient → what the research shows → study.
- **Three studies, each linked once, all in the second half** (CLAUDE.md citation rule).
- **A true "last updated" date** and the author byline.
- **One FAQ** built from the "People also ask" questions recorded in `data/processed/aeo_tracking.csv`.
- **No product claims inside the informational answer.** The product bridge sits after the evidence, within structure/function wording.
