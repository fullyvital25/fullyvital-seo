# Product page brief: search titles, descriptions, FAQs and fixes

**For:** whoever edits fullyvital.com product pages.
**Date:** 18 October 2026.
**Why this matters:** the biggest buying searches in our category are answered by product pages, not blog posts. For example, "hair growth supplements" gets 110,000 searches a month and "hair growth serum" 74,000. Today our product pages rank for almost nothing except our own brand name. The blog has been rebuilt around topic hubs that link to these pages. This brief covers the product-page side.

Search volumes are US monthly figures from DataForSEO. Current rankings are from Google Search Console (90 days to 9 October).

## The five most important changes

1. **Remove "Reverse Grays" from the Anti-Gray Supplements title,** and check the other wording flagged under "Claims to check" below. These are structure/function products, so the wording should be "supports" or "designed to", never "reverse", "stop" or "treat".
2. **Put the search term in each title,** written the way people search (table below) - COMPLETED.

**Please don't add links from product pages to blog posts.** Links go from the blog to products only (house rule).

## Claims to check

These may go beyond structure/function wording. They're your call, but they're the lines most likely to cause a problem with regulators or ad platforms.

- **Anti-Gray Supplements title:** "to Reverse Grays". Remove.
- **Survey percentages in metas:** "95% saw thicker hair in 90 days", "95% saw results in 60 days", "90% saw fewer grays", "85% saw less grays". If these stay, they need to name the source ("in a customer survey") and match the survey exactly. The blog uses "85% / 88%, April 2025 customer survey", but the product pages say 85%, 90% and 95%.
