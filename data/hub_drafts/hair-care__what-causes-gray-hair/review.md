## What changed and why

- Upgraded into the hub for the 'what causes gray hair' cluster (about 47,000 searches a month across variants). Title and meta now cover both 'gray' and 'grey'.
- Added an at-a-glance table: cause, what happens, can you change it. Every cause section now links to its supporting article (stress, cortisol, hydrogen peroxide, catalase, smoking, vitamins, nutrient depletion, copper, thyroid, medications, sudden gray).
- Replaced the 8 references with the 3 strongest studies under your citation rule: a 4,192-person worldwide survey on age of onset, a 2020 systematic review of risk factors, and the 2021 human stress and repigmentation study. All three are linked once, in the references at the end.
- Claims that depended on the dropped sources were made general or removed: the 2009 hydrogen-peroxide study figures, the 2020 mouse stress study, the BMJ smoking letter, the 2017 B12/biotin case-control study, the premature-graying age cut-offs and the PTP-20 lab finding.
- FAQ rebuilt around Google's 'People also ask' questions for this search (average age, stress, sudden graying, vitamin deficiency, how to stop it). FAQPage markup is rebuilt from the visible FAQ.
- Product section kept to structure/function wording. Ingredients named are all in the product sheet (copper, B12, folate, L-tyrosine, glutathione).
- No survey statistic on this page (there wasn't one before either). No reviewer line.
