Why Is Your Hairline Receding On One Side?

There's rarely one single answer. Genetics, styling habits and sleeping position all have some research behind them, and some less common causes need a doctor to identify. The sections below cover the three with the clearest evidence: uneven pattern hair loss, sleeping position and tight styling. Photos taken over time are the easiest way to tell whether a hairline is stable or still moving.

What To Do About It

  • Loosen the tension. If you wear tight ponytails, braids or buns, change the style or the tension.
  • Track it. Take photos in the same light every few weeks so you can see whether it's changing.
  • Don't guess at supplements. No nutrient has been shown to change a one-sided hairline.
  • See a dermatologist for a diagnosis. A full history and exam can sort out the cause, and treatment depends on the diagnosis.

What The Research Shows

Pattern Hair Loss Can Be Uneven

In a study of 41 patients with early androgenetic alopecia, the extent of frontotemporal recession differed subtly from side to side, and most had larger right-sided recession. The authors described the asymmetry as previously unnoticed and said its biological cause is unknown (Azar et al., 2016).

Sleeping Position

A 2026 study of 161 men with androgenetic alopecia and an M-shaped hairline compared their sleeping habits with the area of hairline recession. The ratio of left to right recession (above 1 means more recession on the left) differed by sleeping position: 1.19 for left-side sleepers, 0.90 for right-side sleepers and 1.00 for back sleepers or those with no fixed position. Combing direction made no significant difference (Wu et al., 2026). This is an observational link in men who already had hair loss, not proof of cause.

Tight Hairstyles (Traction Alopecia)

Hairstyles and practices that frequently put tension on the follicle can cause traction alopecia. Recognizing it at its reversible stage matters, because early changes to the style can stop it from progressing to permanent scarring (Afifi et al., 2021).

When To See A Doctor

If the recession is fast, patchy or still progressing, see a dermatologist. Medical options exist for different types of hair loss, so talk to your doctor about what fits your diagnosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a receding hairline on one side grow back?

It depends on the cause. Traction alopecia is reversible at an early stage but can progress to permanent scarring if the pulling continues. A dermatologist can tell which cause you have.

Does sleeping on one side cause hair loss?

In 161 men with pattern hair loss, sleeping position was linked to which side receded more. It's an association in people who already had hair loss, not proof that sleeping position alone causes it.

Does stress cause a one-sided receding hairline?

We found no study linking stress to one-sided hairline recession. If you're losing hair, see a dermatologist to find the cause.
