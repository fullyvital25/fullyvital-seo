What Is Oyster Extract?

Oyster extract is a supplement ingredient made from oysters. Its relevance to hair is its nutrient content, especially zinc.

How To Get Zinc

  • Food. Foods of animal origin are the major dietary sources of zinc, and oysters are outstanding. Lean beef, beef liver, dark poultry meat, egg yolk and cheese are also high.
  • Supplements. Oyster extract is one option. A multinutrient formula can also provide zinc.
  • Get tested. If you're losing hair, a doctor can check for deficiency rather than you guessing.

What The Research Shows

Zinc In Foods

A review of the zinc content of foods found the major dietary sources are animal foods. Oysters, with more than 5 mg of zinc per 3-oz cooked portion, were an outstanding source, alongside lean beef and beef liver. Vegetables, fruits, oils, tea and coffee contain little zinc (Murphy et al., 1975).

Do Supplements Help Hair Loss?

A 2023 systematic review of 30 studies of nutritional interventions in people without a known deficiency found that zinc was among those with the highest-quality evidence of potential benefit, along with several multi-ingredient products. Adverse effects were rare and mild. The authors said the findings should be read in the context of each study's design and called for larger trials (Drake et al., 2023).

Is Low Zinc A Common Cause?

In a retrospective study of 23,975 patients, those diagnosed with hair loss had a median zinc level of 96 µg/dL versus 99 in controls. The authors called the difference minor and clinically insignificant and suggested zinc testing shouldn't be routine when investigating hair loss, while calling for studies on zinc supplements (Liran et al., 2025).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does oyster extract help hair growth?

We found no study of oyster extract for hair. Its relevance is as a zinc source, and the evidence on zinc and hair is mixed.

Are oysters high in zinc?

Yes. A food review found more than 5 mg of zinc in a 3-oz cooked portion, which made oysters an outstanding source.

Is oyster extract safe?

Talk to your doctor before taking it, especially if you have a shellfish allergy, are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a condition or take medication.
