What Are Lignans?

Lignans are plant compounds found in flaxseed. The main flaxseed lignan is secoisolariciresinol diglucoside (SDG), an antioxidant.

Do Flax Seeds Help Hair?

Honest answer: no one knows. Claims that flaxseed stops hair loss aren't backed by studies we could find. Its best-documented effects are on skin hydration and on heart health, which is not the same as hair.

What The Research Shows

What Flaxseed Contains

Flaxseed contains alpha-linolenic acid, lignans and dietary fiber. Reviewers describe benefits including improved lipid profile and lower blood pressure, and note that it may improve skin condition and speed wound healing. It also contains antinutrients, including cyanogenic glycosides, cadmium, trypsin inhibitors and phytic acid. The bioavailability of ALA and lignans depends on the form eaten, and ground seed is recommended (Nowak & Jeziorek, 2023).

A Skin Trial

Women took flaxseed oil, borage oil or a placebo for 12 weeks at 2.2 g of total fatty acids a day. In the flaxseed oil group, plasma ALA rose, and skin hydration increased significantly compared with the start. Skin roughness and scaling decreased significantly, and transepidermal water loss fell by about 10% after 6 weeks and further by 12 weeks (De Spirt et al., 2009). This was a skin study, not a hair study.

The Lignan SDG

Flaxseed's lignan SDG is an antioxidant, and reviews of the cardiovascular research on flaxseed credit it, along with ALA and fiber, for effects such as lower blood pressure and cholesterol. The research is on the heart, not hair (Parikh et al., 2018).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does flaxseed help hair growth?

We found no study showing that it does.

What is the best way to eat flaxseed?

Ground, since ALA and lignans are more bioavailable from ground seed.

Is flaxseed safe?

Flaxseed contains antinutrients such as cyanogenic glycosides and cadmium. Talk to your doctor before taking large amounts, especially if you're pregnant, breastfeeding or take medication.
