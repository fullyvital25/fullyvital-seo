What Is A Widow's Peak?

A widow's peak is a V-shaped point where the hairline reaches down at the center of the forehead. It's a natural feature of the hairline, not a sign that something is wrong.

Widow's Peak Or Receding Hairline?

They can look similar, but they're different. A widow's peak is a feature of your natural hairline pattern. A receding hairline is hair loss. If your hairline is moving back or thinning at the temples, it's worth showing a dermatologist. Knowing your normal pattern is a useful baseline for that conversation.

How To Style A Widow's Peak

There's no single right way to wear one. These are styling ideas, not medical advice:

  • Show it off. A middle part, slicked-back styles and pixie cuts put the peak front and center.
  • Soften it. Side-swept bangs, an off-center part and soft waves or layers that fall across the forehead blend the point into the rest of your hair.
  • Try before you commit. Test a new part or bangs with clips or a headband first, and ask your stylist for advice that fits your hair texture.

What The Research Says

Originally believed to be an omen of early widowhood, the widow's peak is now known to be a morphogenetic trait, and in the majority of cases it is a normal variant (Kyriakou et al., 2021).

How common is it? In a study of 360 women, a widow's peak was present in 81% (Nusbaum & Fuentefria, 2009). In 103 healthy premenopausal Spanish women it was present in 94.17%, with a mean height of 1.01 cm and width of 2.13 cm. The authors noted that knowing normal hairline patterns can matter when evaluating women with hair loss (Ceballos et al., 2013).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a widow's peak normal?

Yes. In the majority of people it's a normal variant, and it was present in most women in two separate studies.

Does a widow's peak mean I'm going bald?

We found no study showing that a widow's peak predicts baldness. Hair loss is a separate change, so if your hairline is moving back or thinning, see a dermatologist.

What hairstyles work with a widow's peak?

It's personal. Side-swept bangs, an off-center part and soft waves soften the peak, while a middle part, a slicked-back look or a pixie cut shows it off.
