What The Research Shows

Skin Effects

A systematic review followed PRISMA guidelines and found 12 studies from the past 10 years of Curcuma species and curcumin on skin. Reported effects included antioxidant and anti-inflammatory activity, increased skin moisture, benefit in psoriasis lesions and radiodermatitis, and reduced axillary hair growth. The authors said more human trials are needed to establish delivery methods and dosages (Barbalho et al., 2021).

General Safety

A review of turmeric and curcumin safety concluded that human studies did not show toxic effects, and that curcumin was safe at 6 g a day orally for 4 to 7 weeks. It noted that gastrointestinal upset may occur, and that there are still few trials (Soleimani et al., 2018).

Liver Toxicity Report

A 2026 letter in the Journal of the American Academy of Dermatology is titled "Liver toxicity, a rare side effect of using turmeric supplements for hair loss" (Paz et al., 2026). The abstract wasn't available to us, so we can only report what its title says: that liver toxicity is a rare side effect of turmeric supplements used for hair loss.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does turmeric help hair growth?

We found no trial showing that.

Is turmeric safe?

General safety reviews found it well tolerated at studied doses, but a recent letter reports rare liver toxicity from supplements. Check with your doctor.

Is turmeric good for the scalp?

The skin research is on other body areas. We found no scalp trial.
