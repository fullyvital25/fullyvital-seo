What Is Rhodiola?

Rhodiola (Rhodiola rosea) is a root used in herbal products. It is often sold as an adaptogen, meaning a substance thought to help the body cope with stress.

Does Rhodiola Help Hair?

No evidence says so. The only link is the indirect idea that stress affects hair, and Rhodiola's effect on stress hormones isn't established.

Using Rhodiola Carefully

  • Treat hair benefits as unproven.
  • Check product quality. A survey of one type of Rhodiola product found many adulterated samples.
  • Ask your doctor first if you're pregnant, breastfeeding, have a condition or take medication.

What The Research Shows

Stress And Fatigue

In a double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study, 56 young, healthy physicians took a standardized Rhodiola rosea extract (SHR-5) or placebo for two weeks during night duty. The treated group had a statistically significant improvement in a combined fatigue index of mental performance tests during the first period, and no side effects were reported (Darbinyan et al., 2000).

Stress Hormones

A systematic review of 52 randomized human studies of plants and their effects on cortisol and related hormones found the overall effect of phytonutrients on HPA-axis activity was unclear, with the most consistent finding being a morning cortisol-lowering effect from ashwagandha. Rhodiola rosea was among the plants examined (Lopresti et al., 2022).

Product Quality

A DNA-barcoding survey of commercial Rhodiola crenulata decoction pieces from drug stores and hospitals found only 36 sequences (40%) were authentic R. crenulata. The rest were adulterants, including R. serrata (35 sequences) and R. rosea (9), which the authors said may indicate a potential safety issue (Xin et al., 2015).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Rhodiola help hair growth?

We found no study showing that it does.

Does Rhodiola reduce stress?

In one small trial it improved mental performance during night duty, but a systematic review found its effect on stress hormones unclear.

Is Rhodiola safe?

No side effects were reported in the trial, but product quality varies. Talk to your doctor first.
