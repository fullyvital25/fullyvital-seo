Using Pea Protein Sensibly

  • Aim for enough protein overall, not a particular source.
  • Persistent shedding is worth a doctor visit. A review notes nutritional factors, such as iron status, can play a role.
  • Avoid excess supplements. The same review says excessive intakes may cause hair loss.

What The Research Shows

Nutrition And Hair Loss

A review of nutritional factors and hair loss concluded that what is known comes from studies of protein-energy malnutrition, starvation and eating disorders. In otherwise healthy people, nutritional factors appear to play a role in persistent increased shedding. It noted that excessive intakes of nutritional supplements may actually cause hair loss and are not recommended without a proven deficiency (Rushton, 2002).

How Well Pea Protein Is Digested

In a randomized trial, 15 healthy volunteers ate meals with either pea protein isolate or casein. Amino acid digestibility was 93.6% for pea protein and 96.8% for casein, with no significant difference. Leucine, valine, lysine and phenylalanine were less digestible in pea. The digestible indispensable amino acid score was 1.00 for pea protein and 1.45 for casein, and the authors concluded pea protein could meet all amino acid requirements (Guillin et al., 2022). That study was about nutrition, not hair.

Pea Sprout Extract

A short communication tested pea sprout extract, not pea protein. A fluid with 2% extract applied to the scalp of 10 volunteers increased expression of two hair-related genes, FGF7 (56%) and noggin (85%). In a pilot study of 21 volunteers, 100 mg a day for 8 weeks significantly reduced hair loss from day 28, with no adverse events reported (Grothe et al., 2020). It was small, and the product was a branded extract.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does pea protein help hair grow?

We found no study showing that.

Is pea protein a good protein source?

In one trial, its digestibility was close to casein's.

Is pea sprout extract the same as pea protein?

No. The pilot study used a sprout extract at 100 mg a day.
