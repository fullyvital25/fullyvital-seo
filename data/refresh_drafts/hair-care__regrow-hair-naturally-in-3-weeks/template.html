What Does Hair Regrowth Involve?

Each hair goes through a cycle. A hair that sheds has to enter a new growth phase, and a new hair has to grow long enough to be seen.

What The Research Shows

The Hair Cycle In Hair Loss

In a study that followed 10 men with or without alopecia by phototrichogram for 15 years, researchers accounted for almost 10,000 hair cycles. In alopecia, a growing proportion of hairs had a shorter growth phase, hair diameter declined, and the gap between a hair falling and regrowth beginning was longer. The authors said the shorter, finer hairs were absent more often and for longer periods (Courtois et al., 1994).

Seasonal Variation

Researchers measured hair growth every 28 days for 18 months in 14 healthy men. The proportion of scalp follicles in the growth phase reached a peak of over 90% in March and fell steadily to a trough in September, when shedding reached about 60 hairs a day, more than double the preceding winter's. The study shows that shedding can rise and fall through the year (Randall and Ebling, 1991).

Timing Of Nutritional Shedding

A review of nutrition and hair loss in women reported that in women the major cause of hair loss before age 50 is nutritional, affecting 30%, with persistent increased shedding as a principal change. The authors noted that correcting it can take many months (Rushton et al., 2002).
