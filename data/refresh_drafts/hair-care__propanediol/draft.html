What The Research Shows

Alkane Diols In Cosmetics

The Expert Panel for Cosmetic Ingredient Safety assessed 10 alkane diol ingredients that are structurally related small diols, most reported to function as solvents in cosmetics. It concluded seven are safe in present practices of use and concentration, and that data were insufficient for three: 1,4-butanediol, 2,3-butanediol and octanediol (Scott et al., 2024). The abstract doesn't list which seven.

2-Methyl-1,3-Propanediol

A toxicology review of 2-methyl-1,3-propanediol found low toxicity by all routes. It is rapidly absorbed through skin and by mouth and excreted in urine. Barely perceptible skin irritation was seen in human volunteers, and patch tests showed minimal response in about 1% of individuals. A 90-day rat study found no adverse effects (Fowles et al., 2017).

Aminomethyl Propanediol

The Cosmetic Ingredient Review panel assessed tromethamine, aminomethyl propanediol and aminoethyl propanediol, which function in cosmetics as pH adjusters. It concluded they are safe in cosmetics in the practices of use and concentration given in the assessment (Becker et al., 2018).

Caprylhydroxamic acid is another cosmetic ingredient with a safety literature of its own; see caprylhydroxamic acid.
