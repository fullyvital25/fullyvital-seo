What The Research Shows

Coconut, Sunflower And Mineral Oil

Researchers compared coconut, sunflower and mineral oils on hair. Coconut oil was the only oil found to remarkably reduce protein loss in undamaged and damaged hair, whether used before or after washing. Sunflower and mineral oils did not reduce protein loss at all. The authors linked the difference to the oils' composition (Rele & Mohile, 2003).

Porosity And Washing

Surfactants in routine washing diffuse into hair and solubilize protein, increasing porosity. Coconut-based hair oils prevented that rise in porosity, restored the drop in break stress and toughness, and reduced color loss in lab measurements (Kaushik et al., 2022).

Oils On Skin

A review of natural oils for skin barrier repair found that differing ratios of essential fatty acids determine the benefit: oils with a higher linoleic-to-oleic acid ratio have better barrier-repair potential, while oils with more irritating oleic acid may be detrimental. Processing matters too, and cold-pressed oils preserve beneficial lipids (Vaughn et al., 2018). That review was about skin, not hair or batana.
