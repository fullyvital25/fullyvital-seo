What The Research Shows

Regrowth In Mice

Researchers applied a 70% ethanolic extract of ginkgo leaves to shaved C3H mice, including mice pretreated with a high-fat diet. The extract promoted hair regrowth. It also inhibited blood platelet aggregation, thrombin activity and fibrinolysis. The authors suggested it could be used as a hair tonic (Kobayashi et al., 1993). That is a 1993 mouse study.

Alopecia Areata In Mice

A study of water-soluble ginkgo leaf polysaccharides in alopecia areata mice found that one fraction increased VEGF and HGF in skin and decreased inflammatory factors in serum. In cells, a purified polysaccharide lowered several inflammation proteins (p-p65, p-IκBα, TNF-α and IL-1β) (Li et al., 2022). This was a purified polysaccharide, not a supplement.

Safety

A review of ginkgo biloba leaf extract described biological, medicinal and toxicological effects. It notes that ginkgo exerts allergy and changes in bleeding time, that three of its components have been shown to be genotoxic, and that no standards or guidelines regulate the constituents or set exposure limits (Chan et al., 2007).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does ginkgo biloba help hair growth?

We found no human study showing that. The evidence is from mice.

Is ginkgo safe?
