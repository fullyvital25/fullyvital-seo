What Is L-Glutamine?

Glutamine is an amino acid you get from the protein in your diet. Cells use it as a fuel, and it's widely studied in cell metabolism.

Why Glutamine Is Of Interest For Hair

Hair follicles are among the body's fast-growing tissues, so the fuel they run on is a fair question for researchers. The studies below look at that question in the lab, not in people taking supplements.

How Might Glutamine Support Hair?

Fuel For The Hair Follicle

Researchers kept human hair follicles alive for up to 10 days and measured what they burned for energy. Glucose was the main fuel. Glutamine was also important, generating about 2.2 nmol of ATP per follicle per hour, compared with about 0.7 for the lipid fuels tested, palmitate and beta-hydroxybutyrate (Kealey et al., 1994).

Stem Cells And Regeneration

In a laboratory study, hair follicle stem cells shifted their metabolism as they developed, including toward glutamine metabolism. Controlling that shift determined whether cells could return to the stem cell niche and whether hair follicles regenerated well (Kim et al., 2020). In a mouse model of wound-induced hair follicle regrowth, higher glutamine metabolism in skin cells went along with better regeneration (Wang et al., 2023).

These are laboratory and animal results. Clinical trials of glutamine supplements for hair in people are still needed.

Getting And Taking Glutamine

Eating enough protein is the simplest step. There's no hair-specific supplement dose, because glutamine hasn't been tested for hair in people. If you're pregnant, breastfeeding, have a condition or take medication, check with your doctor before taking glutamine.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does glutamine help hair growth?

Glutamine is an important fuel for human hair follicles in the lab, and its metabolism is part of how follicle stem cells work. Whether glutamine supplements change hair growth in people hasn't been tested.

Has glutamine been studied for hair loss?

Not in trials. If you're losing hair, see a dermatologist to find the cause.

How much glutamine should I take?

There isn't a hair-specific dose. Talk to your doctor before starting.
