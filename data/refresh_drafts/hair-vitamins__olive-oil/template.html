What The Research Shows

Oleuropein In Mice

Oleuropein promoted proliferation of cultured human follicle dermal papilla cells and increased β-catenin and related markers. Applied to the skin of telogen-phase C57BL/6N mice at 0.4 mg per mouse per day, it accelerated hair-growth induction and increased follicle size, with higher expression of several growth-factor genes (Tong et al., 2015). This was a single compound, not olive oil, in mice.

Olive Oil On The Skin Barrier

In two randomized forearm studies of 19 adult volunteers, with and without a history of atopic dermatitis, olive oil applied twice daily for 4 to 5 weeks significantly reduced stratum corneum integrity and induced mild erythema. Sunflower seed oil preserved integrity and improved hydration. The authors discouraged olive oil for dry skin and infant massage (Danby et al., 2013). It was forearm skin, not scalp.

Herbal Remedies Overall

A review of herbal remedies for hair loss, with search terms including olive oil, rosemary, saw palmetto and others, reported potential benefits in androgenetic alopecia, telogen effluvium and alopecia areata. The authors said many studies had a lack of long-term follow-up, small samples and short treatment, and that larger randomized trials are required (Ahmed et al., 2025). The abstract doesn't separate out olive oil results.
