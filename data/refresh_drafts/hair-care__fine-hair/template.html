"Fine hair" can mean thin individual strands, low density, little volume, or all three. Research with women who describe their hair as fine found it covers more than strand thickness, and studies of ageing show that both strand diameter and density change over adult life.

Key Takeaways

  • In a study of 151 women, four things linked self-described fine hair to expert and instrument assessments: hair density, thickness, resistance and volume.
  • In 1,414 women, hair diameter rose from age 20 to about 40 to 45 and then fell, while density declined after the youngest group.
  • Thinning became more noticeable to women from the mid 40s to the mid to late 50s.
  • In women over 50, thin hairs differed in structure from thick ones, so age-related thinning is not just a smaller version of the same hair.

What Is Fine Hair?

Fine hair usually refers to hair with a small strand diameter, but people also use it for hair that looks flat or sparse. It is a hair type, not a medical condition on its own.

Fine Hair Or Hair Loss?

Fine hair is how your hair is. Hair loss is a change. If your hair has become thinner or sparser, or you see more scalp, a doctor can look for the cause.

Caring For Fine Hair

  • Handle hair gently. This is general advice, not from the studies above.
  • Don't confuse a style problem with thinning. Compare photos over time.
  • Ask your doctor about sudden change. Nutrition and health conditions can affect hair.

What The Research Shows

How Women Describe Fine Hair

A study of 151 Caucasian women combined self-evaluation, expert assessment and instrument measurements (diameter, density, breakage force, flexibility and scalp sebum). Four parameters were shared across all three approaches: hair abundance (density), hair thickness, hair resistance and hair volume. The authors noted that "fine hair" describes a lot more than just physically thin hair fibres, and that ageing also affects it (Bouabbache et al., 2016).

Diameter And Density With Age

In 1,099 Caucasian women with self-perceived hair loss and 315 with no complaint, scalp hair diameter increased from ages 20 to 40 to 45 and then decreased. Density was highest at 20 to 30 and fell thereafter, faster in women who perceived hair loss. The authors proposed a combined "hair amount" metric and concluded that thinning becomes increasingly noticeable from the mid 40s to the mid to late 50s (Robbins et al., 2012).

What Thin Hairs Look Like

A multiscale comparison of thin and thick hairs from Caucasian women older than 50 found that thin hairs had fewer cuticle layers, a rounder shape, a reduced frequency of medulla, and different keratin content in the cortex, along with increased rigidity. The authors concluded that age-related hair thinning is a more profound process than producing a smaller hair, likely involving an "aged hair program" in the follicle (Baltenneck et al., 2022).

Density and strand thickness are measured differently; see hair density.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is fine hair?

Hair with thin strands, though women often also mean low density or volume.

Does hair get finer with age?

In the studies above, strand diameter fell after about 40 to 45 and density declined with age.

Is fine hair the same as thinning hair?

Not necessarily. See a doctor if your hair has changed.
