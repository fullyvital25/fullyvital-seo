What Is A Derma Roller?

A derma roller is a device with fine needles that create small punctures in the skin. It's an at-home relative of the microneedling done in clinics.

What The Research Shows

A Review Of Microneedling For Hair

A review of microneedling in dermatology found it is a minimally invasive procedure using fine needles to make micro-punctures. In its analysis, microneedling alone increased total hair count more than topical 5% minoxidil, and microneedling plus 5% minoxidil increased total hair count more than microneedling alone. The authors noted that few studies test microneedling as a monotherapy and that larger randomized trials are needed (Gupta et al., 2022).

Microneedling Plus Minoxidil

A systematic review and meta-analysis of 10 randomized trials (466 patients) with androgenetic alopecia found that combining minoxidil and microneedling significantly increased total hair count versus minoxidil alone (standard mean difference 1.76), but the increase in hair diameter was not significant. No scarring or serious adverse events were reported in the studies. Differences in rating scales and microneedling methods may have affected the results (Abdi et al., 2023).

A Home-Use Case Report

A 28-year-old man treating alopecia at home with minoxidil spray followed by derma roller microneedling developed a subpericranial and epidural abscess with skull osteomyelitis, and surgery to evacuate it was planned. The report concluded that a scalp hematoma can become infected after procedures such as microneedling without an open wound (Jain et al., 2026). This was a single case.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a derma roller help hair growth?

Trials of microneedling, mostly a clinic procedure combined with minoxidil, show increased hair counts. Evidence for at-home derma rollers on their own is limited.

Can I use coconut oil after derma rolling?

We found no study on this. Ask a dermatologist what to apply to freshly needled skin.

Is a derma roller safe?

Trials report no serious adverse events, but a case report describes a serious infection after home use. See a doctor first.
