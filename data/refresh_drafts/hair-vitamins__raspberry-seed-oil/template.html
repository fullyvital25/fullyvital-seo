Raspberry seed oil is promoted for skin and hair care, and often as a natural sunscreen. We found no study of it on hair. The research is on its composition, its quality when stored, and a study of vegetable oils' sun protection that found raspberry seed oil's protection far lower than the claims.

Key Takeaways

  • Raspberry seed oil contains fatty acids, tocopherols, carotenoids, flavonoids, phytosterols and other compounds, and shows antimicrobial and antioxidant activity.
  • In a strict test, the SPF of raspberry seed oil and four other promoted oils was far lower than the values reported in earlier, controversial studies.
  • Cold-pressed berry seed oils used for cosmetics can oxidize during storage, so quality and storage matter.
  • We found no study of raspberry seed oil for hair.

What Is Raspberry Seed Oil?

Raspberry seed oil (RSO) comes from the seeds of Rubus idaeus and is used in cosmetics and nutrition.

What The Research Shows

What It Contains

A review describes raspberry seed oil as a source of bioactive compounds including fatty acids, tocopherols, tocotrienols, carotenoids, flavonoids and phytosterols, with reported antimicrobial, antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, and uses in cosmetics (Ispiryan et al., 2021).

Sun Protection

A study tested the real UVB protection of 14 virgin vegetable oils sometimes promoted as safe alternatives to sunscreen. The SPF values of the five most promoted oils, including raspberry seed oil, were significantly lower than the values reported in controversial studies. The authors showed the earlier values were overestimated and that the other oils gave no or negligible SPF (Ácsová et al., 2021).

Quality And Storage

Cold-pressed strawberry, raspberry and blackberry seed oils used for cosmetic purposes were bought from three European producers and analyzed after purchase and after 4 and 8 weeks of storage at room temperature for acid and peroxide values and oxidative stability. The study noted berry seed oils are used for skin and hair care (Raczyk et al., 2021).
