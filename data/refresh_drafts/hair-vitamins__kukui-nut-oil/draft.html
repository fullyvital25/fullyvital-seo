Key Takeaways

  • In ex vivo cultured human hair follicles, kukui nut (Aleurites moluccanus seed) oil produced a significant hair-growth effect and raised levels of prostaglandin F2α.
  • The same paper reported a randomized, double-blind, left-right human test in which the oil promoted eyelash growth.
  • In a small trial in people with psoriasis, kukui nut oil did not reduce symptoms significantly more than mineral oil.
  • Aleurites moluccana seeds eaten for weight loss have been associated with toxicity, and high repeated doses of a seed extract killed rats.

What Is Kukui Nut Oil?

Kukui nut oil comes from the seeds of Aleurites moluccanus (also called candlenut). It's traditionally used to promote hair growth and is widely used as a cosmetic ingredient.

Does Kukui Nut Oil Help Hair?

The evidence is early and rests mostly on one paper: follicles in a dish and eyelashes in a human test. Whether it helps scalp hair in people hasn't been shown.

What The Research Shows

Hair Follicles And Eyelashes

Researchers treated ex vivo human hair follicles with kukui nut oil and saw a significant hair-growth effect. The oil elevated PGF2α levels and the expression of AKR1C family enzymes, upregulated proliferation markers (Ki67 and cyclins B1, D1, E1), and activated Nrf2 signalling. A randomized, double-blind, left-right human test confirmed the oil promoted eyelash growth (Kato et al., 2026).

A Skin Trial

Thirty adults with mild, stable plaque psoriasis were randomized to kukui nut oil or mineral oil placebo for 12 weeks. Both groups improved and there was no significant difference between them; no side effects were reported. The authors called it a small pilot (Brown et al., 2005).

Seed Safety

The seeds of Aleurites moluccana (also called "noz da Índia") have been consumed for weight loss, with reports of toxicity. In rats, an aqueous seed extract showed no cytotoxic or mutagenic effects in vitro, but repeated high doses (250 to 750 mg/kg/day) caused mortality, while lower doses (25 to 100 mg/kg/day) for 28 days did not. The authors recommended further longer-term studies (Fukuda de Castilho et al., 2021).

Frequently Asked Questions

Does kukui nut oil help hair growth?

In cultured human follicles it showed a hair-growth effect, and in a human test it promoted eyelash growth. Scalp hair hasn't been tested in people.

Is kukui nut oil safe?
