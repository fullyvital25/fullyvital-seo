It is not something you can grow or "repair" with a product; it is part of the hair fiber itself.

Key Takeaways

  • Each hair strand has three parts: a cuticle (protective outer layer), a cortex (the bulk of the fiber) and sometimes a medulla (a central core).
  • The cortex makes up about 86 to 90% of the fiber by weight and is packed with keratin proteins.
  • Hair's tensile strength is high (150 to 270 MPa) and depends on how fast it is stretched and on humidity.
  • Hair structure varies from person to person.

What Is The Cortex Of Hair?

The cortex is the layer between the cuticle and the medulla. It's made of spindle-shaped cells filled with keratin bundles, and it contains most of the fiber's mass.

What The Research Shows

The Layers Of A Hair

Mammalian hair fibers divide into a cuticle, a cortex and sometimes a medulla. The cuticle is a thin layer of overlapping cells making up around 10% of the fiber's weight. The cortex makes up the remaining 86 to 90%, made of aligned, spindle-shaped cells packed with macrofibril bundles. The medulla is a column of spaces in the centre that becomes more prevalent as the fiber gets thicker (Plowman & Harland, 2018).

How Hair Structure Varies

X-ray diffraction studies of hair from different people, including parents and children and twins, examined how molecular hair structure differs by sex, color and waviness. The cortex contains the major part of the fiber mass, mainly keratin proteins and structural lipids, and the cuticle is a protective layer of dead overlapping cells (Yang et al., 2014).

Strength Of Hair

Human hair has a high tensile strength of 150 to 270 MPa, which depends significantly on strain rate and humidity. Under large tensile extension, the cortex's keratin structure changes, with the unwinding of the alpha-helix and a possible transformation to beta-sheet (Yu et al., 2017).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the cortex of hair?

The middle layer of a hair strand, between the cuticle and the medulla, holding most of the fiber's mass and strength.

Can you repair the cortex?

We found no study showing a product can rebuild a damaged cortex.

What is the medulla?

A central column of spaces in some hairs, more common in thicker fibers.
