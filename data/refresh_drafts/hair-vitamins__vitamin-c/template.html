What Is Vitamin C?

Vitamin C (ascorbic acid) is a water-soluble vitamin found in fruit and vegetables and in supplements.

What The Research Shows

Vitamins And Minerals In Hair Loss

A review summarized the role of vitamins A, B, C, D and E, iron, selenium and zinc in non-scarring alopecia. It stated that micronutrients play an important but not entirely clear role in follicle development and immune function, that the evidence is enormous and conflicting, and that deficiency may represent a modifiable risk factor. The authors said large double-blind placebo-controlled trials are required to determine the effect of specific micronutrient supplementation on hair growth (Almohanna et al., 2019).

Vitamin C And The Skin

A review of vitamin C in skin health explains that normal skin contains high concentrations of vitamin C, which stimulates collagen synthesis and helps protect against UV-induced photodamage. It notes that the efficacy of adding vitamin C to topical products, as opposed to optimizing dietary intake, is poorly understood, and identifies areas where lack of evidence limits understanding (Pullar et al., 2017). It is about skin, not hair.

What Is In Supplements

A market analysis of 106 vitamin C preparations sold online in Germany and the USA found a mean daily dose of 613.9 mg, against a recommended 90 to 110 mg for adults, and full bioavailability reached from doses of 100 to 400 mg. More than three quarters of products exceeded the recommended daily dose and nearly 40% exceeded a potential upper safety level. Some were missing required or recommended warnings, and more than a third contained other active ingredients that might raise overdose risk (Decke and Seifert, 2025). The analysis wasn't about hair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does vitamin C help hair growth?

We found no trial showing that.

Can low vitamin C cause hair loss?

Reviews list vitamin C among micronutrients studied in hair loss, but they call the evidence conflicting.

How much vitamin C should I take?

The recommended daily dose for adults in that analysis was 90 to 110 mg. Ask your doctor about supplements.
