What Is Pineapple's Active Compound?

Pineapple produces bromelain, a group of enzymes containing sulfhydryl groups. It's studied in many areas of health.

Does Pineapple Help Hair?

No evidence says so. Eating fruit is a good part of a balanced diet, but pineapple hasn't been shown to change hair growth, thickness or shedding.

What The Research Shows

What Bromelain Is

A review describes bromelain as a group of enzymes from pineapple with reported anti-inflammatory, anti-diabetic, anti-cancer and anti-rheumatic properties in preclinical and clinical studies, with new formulations being developed to improve bioavailability and stability (Kumar et al., 2023).

Bromelain After Skin Procedures

A systematic review of trials of arnica and bromelain for bruising and swelling after skin procedures included seven trials of oral bromelain. Results were mixed: some randomized trials reported improvement with bromelain (5 of 7). The authors concluded there is insufficient data to support their use and called for more research. No studies on topical bromelain were found (Ho et al., 2016).

Bromelain In Skin Care

A review of botanical ingredients in dermatology and cosmetics groups bromelain among more commonly used botanicals that are briefly considered, alongside arnica, caffeine, ferulic acid and others (Baumann, 2007).
