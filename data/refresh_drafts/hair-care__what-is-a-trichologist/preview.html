A trichologist is a hair and scalp specialist, but the term doesn't always mean a medical doctor. In some places, trichologists are trained outside medicine, and dermatologists who focus on hair use other titles. Knowing the difference helps you choose who to see for hair loss.

What Is A Trichologist?

Trichology is the study of hair and scalp. Trichologists may help with hair and scalp concerns, but training and regulation vary.

Trichologist Or Dermatologist?

For sudden, patchy or persistent hair loss, a doctor can diagnose the cause. A trichologist may help with hair and scalp care, depending on their training.

What The Research Shows

Terminology

A 2018 paper explains that the term trichology originated in 1860 in a London barbershop, and that in 2010 the term dermatotrichologist was proposed for board-certified dermatologists who study the hair and scalp, in contrast to the trichologist, who is rather associated with laity and cosmetics. It proposes "trichiatrist" for the strictly medical professional, and notes that phony hair loss solutions continue to be marketed (Trüeb et al., 2018). This is a viewpoint from dermatologists.

A Trichologist's View

A 2002 article written to establish clinical trichology as a specialty argues it is not an alternative or complementary therapy, emphasizes the scientific base of training, and admits that most trichologists are not medically qualified. It calls for the public and the medical profession to differentiate properly trained trichologists from unqualified practitioners (Mason, 2002).

Checking Credentials

A 2007 editorial in the Indian Journal of Dermatology, Venereology and Leprology is titled "Check if your trichologist is a doctor: need for educating the public." PubMed doesn't include an abstract, so we can only report its title (Mysore and Khopkar, 2007).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a trichologist a doctor?

Often not. Two of the sources note that most trichologists are not medically qualified.

Should I see a trichologist or a dermatologist for hair loss?

A doctor can diagnose medical causes. Ask any specialist about their training.

What does a trichologist do?

They focus on hair and scalp care. Practices vary.
