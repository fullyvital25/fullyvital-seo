Half of the enrolled subjects were female. Subjects took one oral dose of their assigned treatment daily. Assessments were performed at baseline, Day 56 and Day 90. Preliminary 90-day results are presented here.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="PATIENTS/METHODS" NlmCategory="METHODS">The active group showed significant improvement in terminal hair count from baseline in anterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0007) and posterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0005) areas. Compared to placebo, the improvement was also significant in the anterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05) and posterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.03) areas. Anterior vellus hair count improved significantly versus baseline (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0005) and placebo (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01). Posterior vellus hair count showed significant within-group growth (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05). Total hair count was significantly greater than baseline for anterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) and posterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0005) and significantly greater for anterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.005) and posterior (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05) compared to placebo. Total terminal hair counts improved 7-fold, and total hair count improved 12-fold compared to placebo. A significant reduction in hair shedding was observed in the active group versus baseline (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05). No meaningful improvements were observed in the placebo group, and no adverse events were reported.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="CONCLUSION" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">These preliminary results indicate the daily administration of a proprietary saw palmetto (Serenoa repens) extract safely and effectively promotes hair growth in men and women with self-perceived thinning hair.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="TRIAL REGISTRATION" NlmCategory="BACKGROUND">Clinicaltrials.gov identifier: NCT06920758.</AbstractText><CopyrightInformation>&#xa9; 2025 The Author(s).
