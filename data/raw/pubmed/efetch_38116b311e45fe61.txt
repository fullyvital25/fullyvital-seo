Inhibitory effects of some Arctostaphylos plants on melanin biosynthesis.</ArticleTitle><Pagination><StartPage>153</StartPage><EndPage>156</EndPage><MedlinePgn>153-6</MedlinePgn></Pagination><Abstract><AbstractText>The inhibitory effect of 50% ethanol extracts obtained from the leaves of six Arctostaphylos plants (A. patula, A. viscida, A. canescens, A. columbiana, A. nevadensis and A. uva-ursi) (Ericaceae) was tested on melanin biosynthesis. Among them, the extracts of A. patula and A. viscida showed potent inhibition against tyrosinase. The two extracts not only inhibited the production of melanin from dopachrome by autoxidation, but exhibited SOD (superoxide dismutase)-like activity and had a moderate absorbance in the UV-B area.
