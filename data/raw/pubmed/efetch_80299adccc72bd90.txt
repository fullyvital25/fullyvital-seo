It was suggested that Cr shows a beneficial influence upon glucose and lipid disturbances.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="CONCLUSIONS" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">The recent clinical trials provided evidence both in favor and against the importance of Cr in healthy and ill organisms.
