Reactive oxygen species generated during melanin biosynthesis in anagen hair bulb have been implicated in melanocyte apoptosis and hair graying. Extraneous factors, namely environmental pollution, stressful lifestyle, may compound the melanogenesis-induced endogenous oxidative stress.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="AIMS" NlmCategory="OBJECTIVE">We aimed to investigate the role of systemic oxidative stress in causation of premature canities and its correlation with the severity of hair graying.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="SETTINGS AND DESIGN" NlmCategory="METHODS">This was a tertiary care hospital-based cross-sectional study.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="MATERIALS AND METHODS" NlmCategory="METHODS">Consecutive 50 patients with premature hair graying, aged &lt;25 years, and 30 age and sex-matched healthy controls were recruited. Severity of premature canities was graded based on the total number of gray hair on the scalp. Redox status was evaluated in cases and controls, by malondialdehyde (MDA), reduced glutathione (rGSH), and superoxide dismutase (SOD) measurement in serum, by enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="RESULTS" NlmCategory="RESULTS">Serum MDA concentration, an oxidative stress marker, was significantly higher (<i>P</i> &lt; 0.01), while serum rGSH and SOD levels, both indicators of antioxidant potential, were significantly lower (<i>P</i> &lt; 0.0001 and <i>P</i> &lt; 0.01 respectively) in premature canities patients compared to controls. A novel observation was the significant correlation of serum MDA rise and serum rGSH decline with increasing severity of hair graying (<i>P</i> &lt; 0.01 and <i>P</i> = 0.01, respectively).</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="CONCLUSION" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">Systemic redox imbalance is present in premature canities patients, with the severity of hair graying varying in parallel to the degree of oxidative stress. Antioxidants supplementation is likely to yield therapeutic benefit in premature canities.</AbstractText><CopyrightInformation>Copyright: &#xa9; 2020 International Journal of
