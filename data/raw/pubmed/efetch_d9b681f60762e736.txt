Twenty-eight female Sprague-Dawley rats (8&#xa0;weeks old) were randomized into one of the following groups: (i) group (control), shaved; (ii) group (ASI), shaved&#x2009;+&#x2009;ASI (4.14&#xa0;mg/rat/day); (iii) group (ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I), shaved&#x2009;+&#x2009;ASI (4.14&#xa0;mg/rat/day)&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB (48.7&#xa0;&#x3bc;g/rat/day); and (iv) group (ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II), shaved&#x2009;+&#x2009;ASI (4.14&#xa0;mg/rat/day)&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB (325&#xa0;&#x3bc;g/rat/day). On day 42, compared with the control group, while hair density (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, and p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0001, respectively) and anagen ratio (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, and p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) increased in the ASI, ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I, and ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II groups, telogen ratio decreased (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, and p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001, respectively). In the molecular analysis, VEGF, HGF, and KGF-2 increased in the ASI (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01, and p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05, respectively), ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0001 for all), and ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0001 for all) groups when compared to the control group. FGF-2 (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01) and IGF-1 (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) were found to be increased in the ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I and ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II groups. SIRT-1 and &#x3b2;-catenin increased in the ASI (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05 and p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01), ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001 for both), and ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0001 for both) groups. Wnt-1 increased in the ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB I (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001) and ASI&#x2009;+&#x2009;MgB II (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.0001) groups. In conclusion, the combination of ASI and MgB could promote hair growth by regulating IGF-1, FGF, KGF, HGF, VEGF, SIRT-1, Wnt, and &#x3b2;-catenin signal pathways. It was also established that ASI did not affect nail growth, whereas the MgB combination was effective using a higher dose of biotin.</AbstractText><CopyrightInformation>&#xa9; 2022.
