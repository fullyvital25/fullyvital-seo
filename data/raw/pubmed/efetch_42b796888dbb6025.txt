Consumer concern persists regarding potential side effects of this product.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="OBJECTIVE">To identify gaps in the safety information associated with the use of prostaglandin eyelash growth products.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="MATERIALS AND METHODS">Literature searches were performed using PubMed, Embase, and Nexis Uni databases without restriction to publication date, language, or study setting.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="RESULTS">The literature pertaining to bimatoprost for treatment of eyelash hypotrichosis is dominated by industry-sponsored clinical trials. The risk of increased iris pigmentation remains inadequately investigated.
