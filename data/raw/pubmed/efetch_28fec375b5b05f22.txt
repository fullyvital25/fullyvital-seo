In Caucasians only (<i>n</i> = 306), in addition to these trends, irritable bowel syndrome was also significantly associated with PHG (<i>P</i> = 0.010, OR = 2.753). In Asians only (<i>n</i> = 75), history of heart disease in a first-degree relative (<i>P</i> = 0.038) was significantly associated with PHG.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="LIMITATIONS" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">As a survey study, the findings may be subject to recall bias.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="CONCLUSIONS" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">Important associations exist between PHG and family history of PHG, psychiatric history, supplement use, and vitamin deficiencies, providing insight into the pathophysiology and potential comorbidities of
