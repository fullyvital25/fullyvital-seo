Here, mice were fed a low fat (LF), high fat (HF), high fat plus quercetin (HF + Q), or high fat plus red onion extract (HF + RO) diet for 9&#x2009;wks and skeletal muscle <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> isoform expression and DNA methylation were determined. Quantification of various <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> isoforms, including isoforms <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;-a</i>, <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;-b</i>, <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;-c</i>, <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;4</i>, total <i>NT-Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i>, and <i>FL-Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i>, showed that only total <i>NT-Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> expression was increased in LF, HF + Q, and HF + RO compared to HF. Furthermore, Q supplementation decreased <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;-a</i> expression compared to LF and HF, and ROE decreased <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;-a</i> expression compared to LF. <i>FL-Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> was decreased in HF + Q and HF + RO compared to LF and HF. HF exhibited hypermethylation at the -260 nucleotide (nt) in the <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> promoter. Q and ROE prevented HFD-induced hypermethylation. -260&#x2009;nt methylation levels were associated with <i>NT-Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> expression only. <i>Pgc-1&#x3b1;</i> isoform expression may be
