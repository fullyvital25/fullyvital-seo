Milk thistle has antioxidant properties and showed promise in trichotillomania in a prior case report. The goal of the current study was to determine the efficacy and tolerability of silymarin in children and adults with trichotillomania.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="METHODS" NlmCategory="METHODS">Twenty individuals (19 [95.0%] women; 16 adults; mean age, 27.9 [11.5] years) with trichotillomania entered a 12-week, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover study (6 weeks of milk thistle and 6 weeks of placebo with a 1-week wash-out in between). Subjects were assessed with the National Institute of Mental Health Trichotillomania Severity Scale (primary outcome), the Massachusetts General Hospital Hair Pulling Scale, Clinical Global Impression scale, and measures of depression, anxiety, and psychosocial functioning.
