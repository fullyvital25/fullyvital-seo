The aim of our study was to evaluate the efficacy, tolerability, and patient compliance of a treatment with an oral supplement based on arginine, l-cystine, zinc and B&lt;inf&gt;6&lt;/inf&gt; vitamin (Cystiphane<sup>&#xae;</sup>, Laboratoires Bailleul, Geneva, Switzerland) with hair-growth properties, administered 4 times daily, in patients affected by TE.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="METHODS" NlmCategory="METHODS">We recruited 20 patients, aged between 18 and 70 years old, affected by TE. We evaluated the efficacy and tolerability of the treatment both qualitatively by collecting the clinician's opinion through a clinical evaluation and clinical-anamnestic form filled in by the researcher, and quantitatively through global photography and trichoscopy. We collected the patient's opinion through a self-assessment test, at the beginning of the recruitment and after 3 months of treatment.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="RESULTS" NlmCategory="RESULTS">Eighteen patients were evaluated. For what concerns hair quantity, at the control trichoscopy, the mean trichoscopic value had risen to +2.055, whereas for hair diameter the mean trichoscopic diameter value had increased to +1.83. After 3 months of treatment, patients gave an average efficacy opinion of 3.61.</AbstractText><AbstractText Label="CONCLUSIONS" NlmCategory="CONCLUSIONS">The oral supplement has proved effective as an
