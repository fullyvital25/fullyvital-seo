When will I see results?

With our hair regrowth kit - you can see decreased hair shedding within 3-4 weeks, and your hair will feel noticeably healthier. More significant results, including thicker, stronger, and longer hair, become pronounced within 2-3 months of consistent use. The longer you use our hair growth serum and supplement, the better your results.

I’ve tried so many products — how do I know this will work?

We get it — many of our customers felt the same way. Using a hair growth serum and supplement together targets thinning from multiple angles — topically and internally. It's clean, effective, and trusted by thousands of women who finally saw real results.

Can I use Fully Vital alongside Hormone Replacement Therapy?

Yes, our hair growth starter kit are safe to use alongside HRT. There are no known interactions with estrogen, progesterone, or testosterone therapies. As always, we recommend checking with your doctor when making changes to your supplement routine — your well-being comes first.

Will it make my hair greasy or oily?

No, our serum is lightweight and dries off completely by the following morning. Many women also use it to style their hair in the morning.

Are your products natural and free from drugs and chemicals?

Our formulations are 100% natural and free of drugs and chemicals. They are also free of: fragrance, parabens, phthlates, benzoates, formaldehyde.

Will FullyVital work for my hair type?

Yes — our hair regrowth kit is designed to support hair growth for all hair types and textures. While hair looks and feels different across ethnicities, the root causes of thinning affect everyone. Our system targets the scalp and follicles where it matters most — regardless of hair type.

How does menopause affect hair growth?

During menopause, estrogen and progesterone drop, creating a hormonal imbalance that can trigger thinning hair, a wider part, receding hairline — and sometimes unwanted facial hair. At the same time, stress hormones rise, circulation slows, and nutritional needs shift. FullyVital is specifically formulated to support your hair through these changes with a holistic, science-backed approach. Try our hair loss kit for quick results.

My doctor told me my labs are normal. Can FullyVital still help?

Yes. In our customer base, many women see normal labs but still experience thinning. FullyVital helps provide the extra balance and support your body needs to kickstart the hair cycle again.
